About Motor Mate 4x4

Practical upgrades for the way you drive

Motor Mate 4x4 is an Australian-owned 4x4 accessories and automotive service business based in Morley, Perth, Western Australia.

We focus on quality products and practical vehicle solutions to help 4WD owners make informed choices about their upgrades. From suspension and wheels to roof racks and ute accessories, the right setup starts with understanding how you plan to use your vehicle.

Whether you're preparing for off-road adventures, planning a touring trip, upgrading a work ute or improving your everyday driving experience, get in touch with Motor Mate 4x4 to discuss suitable options for your vehicle.
